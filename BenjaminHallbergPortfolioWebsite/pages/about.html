I'm a Technical Designer with a specialisation in Unreal Engine 5, passionate about building the systems and structures that bring game worlds to life.

My work spans racing games, dark fantasy action RPGs, and collaborative industry projects — always with an emphasis on precision, performance, and play. I focus on the space where creative vision meets technical execution: from vehicle physics and AI behaviour, to level construction, gameplay scripting, and documentation.


I studied at House of How, a professional game development studio and education programme in Sweden, where I spent several years working alongside industry veterans and building a deep foundation in technical design. During that time I had the opportunity to collaborate directly with Crystal Dynamics on an industry project.
